Variety is the spice of life

Every character has five variations that rotate as you type, so the same letter never looks the same twice in a row. It's the calt feature and it's on by default.

No touching!

Turn on stylistic set 1 (ss01) and the last letter of every word looks like you touched it before it dried.

  font-feature-settings: "ss01";
